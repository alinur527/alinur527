## Featured Project

### AI Meeting Minutes

A local meeting assistant that turns audio into timestamped transcripts, speaker-separated dialogue, structured summaries and action items. Includes speaker renaming, editable tasks and a meeting confirmation workflow.

Audio → transcription → speaker diarization → summaries & action items → review → DOCX

Started with team Exit 1 at HackAlem and expanded into a standalone engineering project.
